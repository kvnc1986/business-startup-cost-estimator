# Master Startup Cost Categories

Consider all sections and include only relevant lines.

## Formation, legal, tax, compliance
Company incorporation, trade/business name, shareholder/director filings, legal review, accountant onboarding, VAT/sales-tax registration, payroll registration, import/export registration, data-protection registration, operating licenses, sector permits, inspections, certificates, insurance setup, health/safety, food handling, fire inspection, accessibility, public-performance/music licenses.

## Premises
Lease deposit, advance rent, agency fee, legal fee, service/common charges, utility deposits, survey, structural inspection, key money/goodwill, landlord contribution, due diligence.

## Design and fit-out
Architecture, engineering, approvals, demolition, flooring, walls, ceilings, painting, electrical, plumbing, gas, HVAC, extraction, fire suppression, lighting, shopfront, glazing, signage, toilets, accessibility, acoustic treatment, drainage, waterproofing, pest-proofing, waste areas, delivery access, contractor preliminaries, project management, cleanup, snagging.

## Equipment
Create sector-specific lines and include delivery, installation, calibration, warranty, service, spare parts, and import duties where relevant.

## Furniture, fixtures, decor
Counters, shelving, tables, chairs, storage, displays, reception, outdoor furniture, artwork, window treatments.

## IT, systems, security
POS, payment terminals, computers/tablets, printers, router/Wi-Fi, CCTV, alarm, access control, UPS, software, website, domain, email, CRM, booking, inventory, accounting, payroll.

## Opening inventory and consumables
Raw materials, resale stock, packaging, cleaning supplies, stationery, uniforms, safety stock, spare consumables.

## Branding and launch
Naming, logo, brand guide, packaging, signage, menus/catalogues, photography, video, website, social content, ads, launch event, PR, influencer budget.

## People
Recruitment, job ads, background checks, certifications, training, pre-opening payroll, uniforms, payroll taxes, benefits, temporary labor, consultants.

## Finance and payments
Bank fees, merchant setup, payment gateway, financing arrangement fees, interest during fit-out, FX/remittance costs.

## Working capital
Rent, salaries, payroll taxes, utilities, insurance, software, professional fees, stock replenishment, marketing, repairs, cleaning, logistics, subscriptions, debt service, owner salary/draw.

## Contingency
Use a risk-adjusted percentage and explain the basis.
