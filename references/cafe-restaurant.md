# Cafe / Coffee Shop / Restaurant Module

## Premises and compliance
Food-business registration/licensing, health inspection, fire compliance, restroom requirements, accessibility, outdoor seating, music/public-performance licensing, alcohol license if relevant, waste contracts, pest control, grease/waste-oil disposal, grease trap, extraction and ventilation.

## Coffee bar equipment
1/2/3-group espresso machine, primary grinder, secondary/decaf grinder, batch brewer, hot-water boiler, water filtration/softening, refrigerators, ice machine, blender, scales, tampers, knock boxes, pitchers, bar tools, dishwasher/cup washer, sinks, shelving, first spare filters.

## Kitchen / food equipment
Refrigerator, freezer, prep fridge, oven/combi, toaster/panini press, microwave, induction, hood/extraction, stainless prep tables, sinks, dishwasher, smallwares, temperature/HACCP tools.

## Customer area
Tables, chairs, stools, outdoor seating, lighting, menu boards, outlets, Wi-Fi, music system, waste stations, trays, condiment stations.

## Opening stock
Coffee beans, milk/alternatives, tea, chocolate, syrups, food ingredients, pastries, cups/lids, takeaway packaging, napkins, cleaning chemicals, dishwasher chemicals, filters.

## Model
Average ticket, daily transactions, seats/table turns, takeaway share, coffee/food mix, COGS by category, wastage, card fees, delivery-platform commission.

## Often forgotten
Electrical supply upgrades, three-phase power, water pressure, drainage changes, grease trap, extraction route, landlord approvals, espresso installation, water treatment, commissioning, breakages, staff meals, uniforms, menu testing, pre-opening wastage, deep cleaning, pest-control contract, music licensing.
