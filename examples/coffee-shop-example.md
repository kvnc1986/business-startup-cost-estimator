# Example — 80 m² Specialty Coffee Shop

Prompt:
> Estimate the total capital required to open an 80 m² specialty coffee shop in Girne. Assume 30 seats, a two-group espresso machine, one main and one decaf grinder, four staff members, mid-premium positioning, takeaway plus dine-in, and six months of working capital. Research local prices where possible. Give minimum, realistic, and premium scenarios.

Expected behavior:
1. State assumptions.
2. Research commercial rent, equipment, labor, utilities, and local licensing.
3. Build a line-item CAPEX/pre-opening budget.
4. Separate refundable lease deposit.
5. Estimate monthly OPEX.
6. Add 3- and 6-month working capital.
7. Apply justified contingency.
8. Estimate average ticket, gross margin, and break-even traffic.
9. Run sensitivity cases.
10. Identify local data gaps and confidence.
